F.A.Q

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does NexasSolutions Tech specialize in?

We specialize in 6 core areas: enterprise software engineering, AI & custom LLM integration, zero-trust cybersecurity audits, cloud & DevOps infrastructure, custom API middleware, and ODPC/GDPR data protection compliance.

How do you ensure data security and regulatory compliance?

We embed zero-trust security practices into every project. Our compliance leads conduct thorough data protection audits to ensure full alignment with Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) guidelines and global standards.

Can you integrate custom AI models into our existing software?

Yes. Our engineering team designs custom AI workflows, production-ready LLM pipelines, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures that plug directly into your current databases and legacy systems without disruption.

What is your typical project delivery timeline?

Timelines depend on scope, integrations, and project requirements. After discussing your needs, we provide an estimated delivery schedule.

Do you provide post-launch support and infrastructure management?

Absolutely. We offer ongoing maintenance packages including cloud monitoring based on your agreed support plan, CI/CD pipeline automation, security patch updates, and continuous optimization for your software.
